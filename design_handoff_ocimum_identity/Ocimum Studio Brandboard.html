05 — Framing motif

The viewfinder bracket.

${'{{BR}}'}
L-shaped corner marks, as if framing a shot
  • Corner marks appear on section starters, image crops, and framed media — never as a full border. They are drawn in basil green, not cream.
  • On scroll into view, each corner draws itself in via SVG stroke-dashoffset, staggered ~100–150ms corner to corner; a small green anchor dot fades in at each vertex and pulses gently.
  • On hover, the four corners rack outward ~5px, like a lens finding focus. At random intervals, a corner flashes bright green — a focus lock.
  • Depth comes from opacity and hairlines. No drop shadows, no rounded corners, no gradients.

06 — Motion

One curve. Sparse, decisive.

Easing

Every transition uses the same curve — cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) — a fast, decisive settle. No bounce, no linear motion.

Scroll reveals

Content fades in with a 14–20px upward translate over 700–800ms, siblings staggered ~120ms apart.

Restraint

Motion is slow and intentional. If something is always moving, it's wrong. Everything collapses to its final state under reduced motion.

09 — Usage

Do & don't.

Do
  • Keep the accent to a single line, mark, or detail per composition.
  • Let darkness separate sections — generous negative space over dividers.
  • Use hairline borders at low opacity, 1px only.
  • Set micro-labels in tracked uppercase Inter for structure and wayfinding.
Don't
  • Fill a shape or block with the basil green.
  • Use drop shadows, rounded corners, or glassmorphism.
  • Set body copy in Cormorant Garamond, or headlines below Syne 500.
  • Let pure black or pure white enter the palette.
